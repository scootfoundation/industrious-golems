Every golem: who is in charge

The top level, shared by all four golems. The brain's activities pick between these; the task states below say what each task does once it runs.

States

DORMANTNo charge: stands where it is and does nothing. Only the core behaviours (swimming, looking) run.
UNEMPLOYED (start)Charged but without a chest of its own: wanders and looks for a chest to claim as its job site.
SEARCHINGCharged, with a chest. Every few seconds the dispatcher looks for the next thing to do, in a fixed order (see the dispatcher). Strolls near the chest between looks (not the hay golem, who stands still).
DEPOSITINGTask: carrying a stack, so takes it to the chest and puts it in. Always the first thing to do with a full hand.
FETCHING_SUPPLYTask: empty-handed and the work needs a supply, so takes a stack of it out of the chest.
PICKING_UPTask: walks to a stack lying near the chest and picks the whole stack up.
WORKINGTask: the profession's own job (farm, fell trees, dig rock, tend the pen). Which runner has its own diagram.
PANICKINGHurt: runs away. Whatever task was running is dropped.

Visit the chest (put away / fetch a supply)

Any golem. Walks to the spot in front of the chest, faces it, then plays the profession's clip; the lid, the hand-over and the close are each tied to a moment in that clip.

States

APPROACH (start)Walking to the spot in front of the chest (the side it faces). Never starts the clip anywhere else, and not while up on top of the chest.
REACHStanding in front, clip playing, lid still shut.
OPENThe lid is up (the chest is opened for the golem: lid animation and sound). Waiting for the hand to let go or take.
LET_GOPut only: the hand has let go of the stack and it is on its way in.
TRANSFERREDThe stack is in the chest (put) or in the hand (fetch).
THROWNFetch, for golems that fling the stack out onto the grass to be picked up in the usual way.
CLOSEDLid down; the clip is finishing.
DONETask over. Interruptions close the lid and give back any stack the chest never took.

Farmer: reap a crop / sow seeds

Hay golem. Stands a set distance from the plot, facing it, and plays the reap or sow clip; the crop is cut or the seed planted at a moment in the clip. Reaped crops are left to be picked up afterwards.

Lumberjack: fell a tree

Mushroom golem, empty-handed. Walks to the foot of a tree, sneezes, vanishes and reappears on top of the crown, then mines straight down through the trunk. Branches and extra trunks are felled the same way: each time he lands with logs still standing he sneezes up onto the highest. The stump is left for a sapling to be planted in (a separate task, see plant-sapling).

Lumberjack: plant a sapling

Mushroom golem, holding a sapling. Walks to a spot beside the place to plant (the last tree's stump, or open ground near the chest) and plants it. The sapling comes from the hand: fetched from the chest or picked up, never conjured.
